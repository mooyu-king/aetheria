How to play Asciilands online

Use the game frame above to start the independent Quietwater solo edition in your browser. There is no separate desktop installer: press Start game, let the initial assets load, then follow the in-game prompts. Keep the game open while you explore, gather supplies and build your camp.

This page is for browser play, not the original shared multiplayer world. If you want the original Asciilands towns, trading and multiplayer features, visit the creator’s Asciilands page. The solo edition here has its own browser save.

If the frame stays blank, use the direct game link. You can return to the guide for controls and crafting help without restarting your session. Export your save inside the game before clearing browser data or switching devices.

01 / READ THE LANDSCAPE

Learn the controls.
Explore the ASCII world.

ASCII is usually associated with lines of text. Here, characters form a spatial landscape: terrain, trees and structures become places you can walk around. Start slowly and connect a visible resource marker with what happens when you approach it.

Walk with WASD, run with Shift and gather nearby resources with E. If nothing happens, move closer and read the feedback before trying again. A distant or depleted resource is not ready to collect.

Choose a short route you can retrace. Your first objective need not be a complete survey of the island; a familiar gathering spot and a clear return path give your next expedition a purpose.

02 / A LITTLE PREPARATION

Turn a handful of supplies
into your next possibility.

Open the pack with Tab and check what you actually collected. Seeing a material in the world is not the same as carrying it. Let a recipe tell you what to gather next instead of collecting without a destination.

Crafting is a chain of decisions: ingredients first, then the right station, then the item you need. Keep useful raw materials available for construction instead of converting every stack as soon as you return.

If a recipe is unavailable, check three things in order: the quantities in your pack, your distance from the station and whether a required tool is equipped. A tool sitting in the inventory may not meet the same condition as one you are using.

Use the crafting trouble-shooter →
03 / SOMEWHERE TO RETURN

Build a camp.
Give the journey a center.

Open construction with B. Start with an accessible position rather than a crowded layout. Leave room to stand near your stations, move between structures and approach your camp from the gathering route.

A workbench is both a practical crafting point and a useful landmark. Place the next structure only after you understand how the first one fits into your return path. The goal is a base you can use comfortably, not a collection of buildings squeezed into every open space.

Before leaving a session, use Save & help inside the game to export a backup. Your browser world is separate from the website and from the original multiplayer service. Clearing site data, changing browser profiles or moving to another device can affect access to it.

BEFORE YOUR NEXT EXPEDITION

Asciilands online: playing questions

Can I play Asciilands online on this page?

Yes, this page embeds the independent Quietwater solo edition for browser play. Press Start game in the frame above. If browser restrictions prevent the embed from loading, use the direct game link below it. This is not the original multiplayer server.

Is Aetheria Online the name of this game?

Aetheria Online is this independent website. The embedded game is Quietwater, an independent single-player Asciilands development edition. The site name does not imply that the embedded edition has online multiplayer.

Does online play here mean multiplayer?

The embedded solo edition does not connect to the original multiplayer world. For towns, shared-world play and a player economy, read the original Asciilands listing linked below. Those features are not provided by this website.

What are the controls for this edition?

Use WASD to move, Shift to run, E to gather, Tab to open the pack, B to open construction and J to inspect the journal. Check the game’s own help for the current interaction context.

Where is my progress saved?

The solo game saves in browser storage on its game-host origin. This website does not provide cloud saves. Use the game’s export and import tools to transfer a backup, and keep the exported file somewhere you can find it.

Can I play without installing a desktop game?

You do not need a separate desktop installer. The browser still downloads game assets when you start, and the first load can take time. The website loads the game only after you choose to start.

Can I play Asciilands online on mobile?

This site adapts to narrow screens, but that does not prove compatibility of the embedded game on every phone. A desktop browser with keyboard and mouse is the clearest starting point. Device-specific play remains to be checked.

Why does crafting not work?

Look at the recipe, check the actual inventory, equip the required tool and stand near the relevant station. If a material is visible on the map but has not been gathered, it does not count as an ingredient.

What if the game stays blank?

Open the game directly using the link below the player. Browser restrictions or a host embedding policy may prevent an iframe from displaying. Avoid repeatedly reloading an active game; back up progress first if its menu is accessible.

Are the videos recordings of this solo edition?

The two videos are user-selected external references. Their exact version and contents have not been independently verified here; they are not evidence that every shown feature exists in the embedded solo build.
