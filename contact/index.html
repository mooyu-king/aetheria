Add the website to your home screen or desktop. The external game and videos still need an internet connection.

We cannot recover a deleted local save, manage Aetheria Online MMORPG accounts or change YouTube availability. Original multiplayer questions belong with the original project through its itch.io listing. Keep an exported backup of your Emberlight adventure before making changes to browser storage.
