Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026. This policy describes the website at aetheriaonline.online.

Website analytics

We load a Plausible-style analytics script from data.1back.link/js/app.js and send visit events to data.1back.link/api/send using this website’s domain. Requests can include technical request information and page/referrer details. We do not claim to have independently audited the provider’s server retention or processing configuration.

Game and video providers

Starting the game loads s.pokemonsurvivor.wiki in an iframe. Video preview images load from i.ytimg.com as you approach the video section. Playing a video connects to youtube-nocookie.com; following a YouTube link opens YouTube. These services receive network requests, including an IP address and browser information, and may apply their own storage and privacy practices. Privacy-enhanced video embedding does not mean no data is transmitted.

Installed app and offline pages

Installing adds this website to your device. A service worker caches public website assets and recently visited pages on this site. External games, YouTube and analytics are not cached for offline use. Your browser can remove this cache. Installation does not back up or synchronize game saves. We record installation availability, clicks, browser results, help, link copying, app visits and worker errors with the existing analytics service, without adding copied addresses or personal details to event properties.

Local progress and checklist

The game stores its own progress on its host origin. The guide cannot inspect or export that save. Our checklist stays in page memory and resets on reload. We do not provide account registration, cloud synchronization or a payment service on this site.

Contact and choices

If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to send. Do not send passwords or private save data. You can decline to start embedded media, use browser privacy controls and delete local data; export any game backup before deleting storage. For privacy questions or requests about information sent to us, contact marlonqiu2027@gmail.com. Third-party data requests should also be directed to the relevant provider.
